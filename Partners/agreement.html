This Partner Referral Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between Hali Sarah Parsons, an independent beauty consultant based in Atascocita, Texas (“Consultant”), and the business named below (“Partner”).

1. Purpose

Partner agrees to share Consultant's free skin assessment with its customers using the flyers, QR code and referral link Consultant provides. In return, Consultant pays Partner a referral commission as described below.

2. Referred Customers

  1. A “Referred Customer” is a person who reaches Consultant through Partner's flyer, QR code or referral link and selects or is matched to Partner's business name, and who was not already a customer of Consultant.
  2. The referral date is the date the customer first selects Partner. If a customer is referred by more than one partner, the first partner on record receives credit.

3. Commission

  1. Consultant will pay Partner twenty percent (20%) of all sales made to each Referred Customer during the first ninety (90) days after that customer's referral date.
  2. “Sales” means the amount the Referred Customer actually pays for products, not including sales tax, shipping, or amounts later refunded, returned or charged back. Commission on a refunded sale may be deducted from a later payment.
  3. No commission is earned on sales after the 90-day period ends for that customer.

4. Payment

  1. Commission is paid once per Referred Customer, after that customer's 90-day window ends (90 days from the date they were referred). Payment is sent within 7–10 business days after the window ends, using the payment method Partner selected at sign-up (CashApp, PayPal, Zelle, check or another agreed method). Mailed checks may take longer to arrive.
  2. Example: if a customer you referred places a $305 order during their 90 days, Partner earns $61.00 (20%).
  3. Partner will receive an email when each Referred Customer's 90 days are complete, showing the amount to expect.
  4. Partner may request a summary of its Referred Customers' qualifying sales at any time. Customer personal information will not be shared.
  5. Partner is responsible for its own taxes and may be asked for a Form W-9. Consultant will issue tax forms where required by law.

5. Partner Responsibilities

  1. Partner will describe the assessment honestly and will not make medical claims or promises about results.
  2. Partner will not alter the provided flyers' QR code or links, and will not create materials using any company's trademarks, logos or product images.
  3. Partner will not offer customers cash or gifts in exchange for selecting Partner's business.

6. Independent Relationship

This Agreement is only between Consultant and Partner. Consultant is an independent beauty consultant, not an employee or agent of any product company, and no product company is a party to or responsible under this Agreement. Partner is an independent business and not an employee, agent or representative of Consultant.

7. Term and Ending the Agreement

This Agreement starts when both parties sign and continues until either party ends it by written notice (email is fine). Commissions earned on sales made before the end date will still be paid. Consultant may update the program terms for future referrals with 30 days' written notice.

8. Entire Agreement

This is the complete agreement between the parties about the partner program. Changes must be in writing and agreed to by both parties. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
